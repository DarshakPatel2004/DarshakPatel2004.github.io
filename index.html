01 · Jun 2026 to now

DroidForensix

An 18 step static pipeline that links Android malware to its command and control infrastructure and checks its own results against ground truth. One APK in, one JSON report out, with a FastAPI and React dashboard on top. No emulator needed.

97.3%holdout accuracy, 292 of 300 APKs
64.2%family exact match, 352 samples
18pipeline steps
2.4%raw indicators that were real C2, 9 of 380
  • A 15 feature random forest on static features only. The 0.7 threshold was tuned on a separate 100 APK validation split. On a fresh holdout with no hash overlap it caught 146 of 150 malware samples and passed 146 of 150 benign ones (95% CI 94.8 to 98.6).
  • Family attribution in three layers: hand written signatures, a knowledge base matcher mined from corrected ground truth, and an LLM that picks among candidate families. Checked against VirusTotal and MalwareBazaar.
  • Audited C2 extraction by hand: 68% of raw indicators were clearly benign (Firebase, Google OAuth, CDNs).

Family attribution is right about two times in three, and most raw indicators are not C2. Benign filtering and honest reporting are central to the design.

The background dots are decoration. They are not real indicators or locations.
