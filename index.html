HiIntroduction

Hello, I'm Darshak.

I'm studying for an M.Sc. in Digital Forensics and Information Security at the National Forensic Sciences University. Most of my time goes into Android malware: taking samples apart, pulling out the servers they call home, and checking what is actually there.

Before that I did forensics and data recovery at Cyber First in Pune, and spent a summer with IFSO at Delhi Police looking at APK-based fraud. I have worked on phones and computers, and I now work on the infrastructure behind the malware.

This site is written as a lab notebook on purpose. Each entry shows the method and the numbers, and says where they are weak, so you can check my work instead of taking my word for it.

DroidForensix

A static pipeline that pulls command-and-control indicators out of Android malware samples, enriches them with threat intelligence, and maps where they point.

1,711C2 indicators
277samples
203unique IPs
12countries
  • Extracted with AndroGuard, enriched with VirusTotal, OTX, Shodan and Censys.
  • Clustered infrastructure by geography across 26 location clusters.
  • Quick and Full PDF reports, plus an interactive Leaflet map.

Raw indicators, not confirmed C2. Most extracted indicators need verification before they count as attribution.
